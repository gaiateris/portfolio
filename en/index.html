About me

Explosive by nature.

I’m Gaia, Art Director Marketing. I lead the creative and brand vision across several markets — Italy, Spain, France, Portugal, the Netherlands and Benelux — building a cohesive creative direction across Europe. For me creativity isn’t just a job: it’s how I see the world, solve problems and connect with people.

I have the courage to dive into any challenge: from the most complex campaign to the wildest concept, I always put my heart into it, with a touch of irreverence. Outside of work I do pole dancing and live for cinema and music.
